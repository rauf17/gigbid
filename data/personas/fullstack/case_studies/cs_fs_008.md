# Case Study: Automated Customer Support Helpdesk Portal

**Client Industry:** SaaS  
**Date:** 2023-09-08  
**Primary Technologies:** React, WebSocket, FastAPI, Redis, Tailwind  

## The Challenge
Support conversations were fragmented across email, WhatsApp, and in-app chat.

## The Solution Architecture
Centralized messaging onto unified WebSocket queues with typing indicators and automated macros.

## Measured Outcomes & Impact
- **Quantified Result:** Enabled 40 support agents to resolve 350 tickets daily with unified omnichannel chat
- Full unit test suite and automated linting included.
- Zero production regression defects observed.
