# Case Study: Automated Content Scheduling & Social Publisher

**Client Industry:** Marketing  
**Date:** 2023-12-18  
**Primary Technologies:** React, Node.js, Redis, PostgreSQL, AWS SQS  

## The Challenge

## The Solution Architecture
