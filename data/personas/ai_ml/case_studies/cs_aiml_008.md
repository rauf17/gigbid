# Case Study: Speech-to-Text Clinical Scribe

**Client Industry:** HealthTech  
**Date:** 2023-12-11  
**Primary Technologies:** Whisper, PyTorch, FastAPI, Docker, Python  

## The Challenge
Physicians spent up to 3 hours every evening dictating chart notes and medical summaries.

## The Solution Architecture
Fine-tuned Whisper on medical lexicon and structured transcribed outputs into standard SOAP formats.

## Measured Outcomes & Impact
- **Quantified Result:** Reduced clinician note-taking time by 60% with 96.5% medical entity accuracy
- Full unit test suite and automated linting included.
- Zero production regression defects observed.
